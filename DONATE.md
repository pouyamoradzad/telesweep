## ❤️ Support TeleSweep

TeleSweep is free, open source, and will stay that way. It has no ads, no
premium tier, and no telemetry — nothing is ever sold.

If it saved you time cleaning out years of dead chats, a tip is a kind way to
say thanks and help cover development.

### Other ways to help

Money is far from the only way to support the project — and honestly, these
matter just as much:

- ⭐ **Star the repository.** It costs nothing and directly helps others find
  the tool.
- 🐛 **Report bugs** and request features in [Issues](https://github.com/pouyamoradzad/telesweep/issues).
- 🌍 **Translate** TeleSweep into your language — it is a single JSON file
  (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- 📢 **Tell a friend** who is drowning in dead Telegram chats.

Thank you for keeping the internet a little tidier. 🧹
